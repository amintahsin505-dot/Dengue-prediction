SYSTEM SPECIFICATION

System Architecture, Vector Biology & Compliance

Complete technical specification detailing the pipeline data flow, chronological validation split, computational cost efficiency, and mandatory AI disclosures in accordance with the EDU AI Engineering Hackathon 2026 Rulebook.

1. Biological Vector Rationale

Dengue prediction in urban Bangladesh cannot be formulated as a same-day weather correlation problem. The transmission biology of Aedes aegypti dictates a multi-stage time lag:

  • Mosquito Maturation Lag (7–14 days): Female mosquitoes lay eggs in artificial stagnant water containers, rooftop tanks, and plastic-blocked surface drains. Heavy rainfall fills these micro-habitats, requiring 7 to 14 days for eggs to transition through four instar larval stages and pupae into biting adult mosquitoes.
  • Extrinsic Incubation Period (EIP, 8–12 days): Once an adult female takes a viremic blood meal, the dengue flavivirus must replicate across the mosquito midgut and migrate to its salivary glands before transmission can occur.
  • Intrinsic Human Incubation (4–10 days): Following an infectious bite, human viremia develops, leading to fever, platelet drop, and subsequent hospital admission.

The 2-to-4-Week Early-Warning Window: By calculating grouped multi-week rainfall lags (rainfall_accum_2_4w) combined with drainage bottleneck indices (water_logging_risk_index), DENGUARD empowers city corporations to deploy larvicide before adult mosquitoes emerge, stopping the transmission chain at its root.

2. Model Architecture & Selection Rationale

We deployed HistGradientBoostingRegressor models separately tuned for 2-week and 4-week outbreak horizons:

  • Non-Linear Environmental Thresholds: Mosquito mortality increases exponentially above 34°C, while viral replication ceases below 18°C. Tree-based boosting naturally captures these non-linear thresholds and complex feature interactions without fragile polynomial transformations.
  • Missing Value & Skew Robustness: Historical epidemiological datasets exhibit high zero-inflation during dry winter months and extreme right-skewed surges during peak monsoon seasons. HistGradientBoosting natively handles binned features and extreme outliers with L2 regularization.
  • Ultra-Fast Local Serialized Inference: By compiling trained models to compact .joblib artifacts (under 150 KB each), inference on all 60 wards executes in < 50 milliseconds on standard CPU hardware with zero external API calls or latency bottlenecks.
